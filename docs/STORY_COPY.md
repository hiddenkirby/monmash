# Story Copy

Issue: #144

This is the reviewable copy source for v0.7 story, zone guidance, goals, and Old Barnaby
moments. Keep lines short, warm, concrete, and easy to read aloud.

## Voice Rules

- Invite instead of instructing.
- Point to places and discoveries, not scores.
- Avoid scolding words such as `wrong`, `failed`, `locked`, and `blocked`.
- Keep most lines to one sentence.
- Use signs for place hints and Mira for moments that should feel personal.

## Review Checklist

- Warm, short, and non-scolding.
- No protected franchise references.
- No timers, scores, stars, streaks, currency, or loss language.
- Ready for Ryan review before implementation locks the copy.
